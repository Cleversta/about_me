About

I taught myself to build software by building software — no bootcamp, no classroom. Just curiosity, a lot of broken builds, and enough stubbornness to fix them at 2am.

Photo
marason-1.jpg

I'm happy when something I build makes someone's daily life a little easier. That's really the whole point for me — useful apps for everyday problems, nothing more complicated than that.

I got tired of opening an app just to be met with ads, or realizing the “free” app I'm using is quietly making money off me one way or another. So I decided to build the opposite — apps that are completely free, for everyone, with nothing hidden behind them.

100% free

No paid tier required to use the apps. No subscriptions locked behind a wall.

No ads

No banners, no video ads, no “watch to unlock.” Your attention stays yours.

Built solo

Designed, coded, and shipped alone — so the product can stay honest and simple.

KeepMemo

Most apps help you manage contacts. KeepMemo helps you remember people — their birthday, what they love, the gifts you've given them, the moments you've shared, and how those moments felt. Save memories as photos, voice notes, or writing, tag them to the people they're about, and let “On This Day” quietly resurface them a year later, like finding an old letter. Everything lives only on your phone — no server, no account, no data collection.
